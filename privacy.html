KidsRewards — Privacy Policy

Your child's privacy matters. Here's exactly how we handle data.

📅  This Privacy Policy is effective as of 30 August 2026.
This Privacy Policy explains how gunavs ("we", "us", "our", the "Service Provider") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Kids Rewards mobile application (the "App"). Kids Rewards is a family app that lets a parent or guardian reward children for chores with points, add monthly pocket money, and offers redeemable rewards, dream goals and a Piggy Bank savings feature. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. This policy is written to serve families in Australia, India, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom and the United States, and includes region-specific rights in Section 9.

1. Information We Collect

▼
🔒 We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

We collect only what is needed to operate the App:

a) Parent/guardian account (Google Sign-In). To secure the App, a parent or guardian signs in with their Google account. Through Google Sign-In we receive your name, email address and basic Google profile (using the "email" and "profile" scopes). We use this only to identify the account holder and enable sign-in.

b) Children's information you enter. As a parent or guardian, you may add one or more children ("Explorers") and enter their name or nickname, an emoji or photo avatar, an optional date of birth, and their points and Piggy Bank balances, activities, rewards, dream goals and transaction history. This information is created by you and stored locally on your device (see Section 3).

c) A local award password. If you choose to set one, an optional award password is stored locally on your device to gate awarding points and Piggy Bank transfers. It is a local gate, not an online account credential.

d) Automatically collected data. Like most mobile apps, the App and its underlying platform services (Google Play Services and Firebase) may automatically collect limited technical data such as device type, device identifiers, operating-system version, app-version and diagnostic/usage information. This is used for stability, security and to improve the App.

🎯

2. How We Use Information

▼

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate and maintain the App's reward, Piggy Bank and profile features;
  • Authenticate the parent/guardian and keep the App secure;
  • Save your family's progress, points and history on your device;
  • Diagnose problems, prevent abuse and improve performance and reliability;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use children's information for advertising or profiling, and we do not build marketing profiles of children.

💾

3. Where Data Is Stored & Retention

▼
📴 Your family's data is stored locally on your device — not on our servers.

All information about your children (profiles, points, Piggy Bank balances, activities, rewards, dream goals and transaction history) is stored in a private database on your device. We do not operate a cloud database and we do not upload this information to our servers.

Retention & deletion. Because the data is on your device, you are in control. You can delete an individual child, clear entries, or remove all data by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling the App removes the local database from your device. Your Google account information is managed by Google under Google's own privacy policy; you can review or revoke the App's access from your Google Account settings.

🔗

4. Third-Party Services

▼

The App relies on a small number of trusted third-party services. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In & Google Account — authentication.
  • Google Play Services — core app services on Android.
  • Google Firebase — app stability, security and basic usage analytics on Android.

We may disclose information only where necessary: to comply with the law or a valid legal request; to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us; or to detect, prevent or address fraud, security or technical issues.

👨‍👧

5. Children's Privacy

▼
🔔 This section is especially important — Kids Rewards is designed to be used by a parent or guardian on behalf of their children.

The App is intended to be operated by a parent or legal guardian. Children do not create accounts or sign in. All account management and data entry is performed by the adult, who provides consent for any children's information entered into the App.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and we do not require a child to provide information beyond what the parent chooses to enter (such as a name or nickname), which stays on the device. We do not sell children's information or use it for targeted advertising.

If you believe a child's information has been provided without appropriate parental consent, or you would like it removed, please contact us at veereshmsv@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action. Because the data lives on your device, deleting the child in the App or uninstalling the App also removes it.

We honour the children's-data protections that apply in the regions we serve, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age-Appropriate Design Code, and the parental-consent requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. See Section 9 for details.

📱

6. Device Permissions

▼

The App may ask for the following permissions, which you can grant or deny:

  • Camera / Photos. If you choose to add a photo avatar for a child, the App accesses your camera or photo library. Selected photos are copied into the App's private storage on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Internet. Used for Google Sign-In and core Google/Firebase services. The App otherwise works offline.

The App does not collect precise GPS location.

🔒

7. Data Security

▼

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. Because your family's data is stored locally, its security also depends on your device — we recommend using a device lock screen and keeping your operating system up to date.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We will handle any personal-data breach in line with the applicable laws of the regions we serve.

⚖️

8. Your Privacy Rights

▼

Subject to your local laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because most data is stored on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly within the App (edit or delete a child, clear data, or uninstall). For anything else — including your Google account data — contact us at veereshmsv@gmail.com and we will respond within the timeframe required by your local law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Country-Specific Notices

▼

The following notices supplement this policy for users in the regions we serve. Where a regional notice conflicts with the general policy, the regional notice governs for users in that region.

🇦🇺 Australia

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). You may request access to and correction of your personal information and make a privacy complaint by emailing us. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au. We do not disclose your personal information to overseas recipients except through the third-party services listed in Section 4.

🇮🇳 India

We handle personal data consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). The parent/guardian acts as the account holder and provides verifiable consent for the processing of any child's data entered into the App; we do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children. As a Data Principal you may access, correct, update, complete or erase your data and may nominate or raise a grievance. To exercise these rights or to reach our grievance contact, email veereshmsv@gmail.com. You may also escalate to the Data Protection Board of India once operational.

🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates

We handle personal data consistent with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL). We process personal data on the basis of your consent and for the legitimate purpose of operating the App. You have the right to obtain information about your data, request correction or erasure, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at veereshmsv@gmail.com.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

We process personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and we follow the ICO's Age-Appropriate Design Code for services likely to be accessed by children. Our lawful bases are your consent (which you may withdraw at any time) and our legitimate interests in operating and securing the App. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We are the data controller; contact veereshmsv@gmail.com.

🇺🇸 United States

Children (COPPA). The App is operated by parents/guardians, who provide consent for any children's information entered. We do not knowingly collect personal information online directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent, and children's information is stored locally on your device. A parent may review or delete their child's information in the App or by contacting us.

California (CCPA/CPRA) & other state laws. We do not sell or "share" (for cross-context behavioural advertising) your personal information, and we do not knowingly do so for minors. You have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. To make a request, email veereshmsv@gmail.com.
