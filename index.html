The Piggy Bank

Teach saving, the fun way

Birthday money, festival gifts and special bonuses don't have to be spent right away. The Piggy Bank gives every explorer a savings jar of their own.

  • ✓Create gift events for birthdays, Diwali, Eid, Christmas and more.
  • ✓Log gift points into a separate savings balance, kept apart from spendable points.
  • ✓Move savings to spendable whenever your child is ready for a bigger reward.
  • ✓Watch savings grow — a gentle first lesson in money and patience.
🔐

Your family's data stays with your family
