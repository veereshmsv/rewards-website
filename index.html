Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is Kids Rewards?
Kids Rewards is an Android app that helps parents turn everyday chores into a fun, motivating adventure. You award points for good deeds, add monthly pocket money, and kids redeem points for real rewards you choose or save them in a Piggy Bank — all while kids learn the basics of money.
Can I give my child monthly pocket money?
Yes. It's not just chores — Kids Rewards lets you add monthly pocket money for each explorer, alongside the points they earn for good deeds. Kids can then spend it on rewards or save it in the Piggy Bank, a real-world lesson in managing regular income.
Is Kids Rewards free?
Yes. Kids Rewards is free to download on Google Play and is family-friendly.
Which devices does Kids Rewards support?
Kids Rewards is available for Android devices on the Google Play Store.
Is my family's data private, and does the app work offline?
Yes. Your explorers' information is stored locally on your device rather than on our servers, and Kids Rewards works without an internet connection. We never sell or rent your data.
How does the Piggy Bank work?
The Piggy Bank is a separate savings jar for each child. Birthday money, festival gifts and bonus points can be logged there, kept apart from spendable points, and moved to spendable whenever your child is ready for a bigger reward.
Does Kids Rewards help kids learn about money?
Yes. By earning points for chores, redeeming them for rewards and saving in the Piggy Bank, kids learn the basics of money — how it's earned, saved and spent — in a hands-on, everyday way.
How do I download Kids Rewards?
Tap any "Get the app" or "Google Play" button on this page, or search for "KidsReward" on the Google Play Store, to install it on your Android device.
